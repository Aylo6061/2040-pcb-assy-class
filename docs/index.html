What is this class about?

This is a hands-on class about all kinds of electronic soldering - surface mount and through hole soldering will be covered, including tips for tricky leadless components like QFNs. A brief lecture will be followed by a demonstration and then you will build your own open source programmable macropad to take home!

What will I learn?

Take this class if you want to learn how to solder surface mount components, or if you just want a refresher. We will also cover through hole soldering. There will be plenty of time to ask questions and to practice!

What will I make?

Not only will you get to practice soldering, you will also make an RGB backlit macropad with a dial and a screen!
